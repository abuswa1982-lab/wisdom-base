Decision records

Topics

A topic is the process a decision belongs to. Everything that is part of one job should share one topic, so it shows up as a single branch on Find. Tick the ones that belong together, type the name you want, and press Merge.

Conditions library

The fixed set of questions your records are built from. Define a question once here and every record that uses it shows the same wording and the same answers. Change it here and it changes everywhere.

Not decided yet

Every unsettled policy question, from every rule, in one place. Settle it and press Settled and the yellow note disappears from that rule. You never have to dig into the rule to remove it.

Outcomes that go nowhere

Decisions with no script and no next step. A rep reaching one gets a verdict and then silence. This is the chain you still have to finish.

Unanswered questions

Questions people asked that no approved record covered. This is your build queue.
